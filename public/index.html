Draw it. Guess it. Win it.

About

Scrawl is a free online multiplayer drawing and guessing game. Each round, one player picks a word and draws it while everyone else tries to guess it in the chat. Guess quickly to earn more points. The player with the most points at the end wins!

How to play

  1. When it's your turn, choose one of the words and draw it before time runs out.
  2. When someone else is drawing, type your guess into the chat.
  3. The faster you guess, the more points you get. The drawer earns points for every correct guess.
  4. Letters are revealed over time as hints.
